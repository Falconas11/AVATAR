AVATAR Pilot Study

Research Participation and Image Processing Information

You are invited to participate in a pilot study of AVATAR, an academic research prototype developed at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

What you will do

You will upload a portrait, enter profile information, generate an age-progressed image, and complete a short feedback questionnaire.

Use of your image

Your uploaded image will be used only to generate an age-progressed portrait for this academic pilot study. The image will be transmitted to the AVATAR system and an external image-generation service for processing.

The research team will not intentionally retain your original or generated image after the generation session. Your image will not be published, presented, or included in research materials without your separate explicit permission.

Please upload only a photograph of yourself or an image that you have permission to use.

Risks and limitations

Uploading a facial image involves a potential privacy risk. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when data is transmitted through online and third-party services.

The generated portrait is an experimental visualization. It is not a medical diagnosis or a certain prediction of how you will look in the future.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may decline to participate or stop at any time without penalty.
